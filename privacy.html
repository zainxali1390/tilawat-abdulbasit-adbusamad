Privacy policy

Tilawat Abdul Basit Abdul Samad · last updated 23 November 2026

This app plays the recitations of Sheikh Abdul Basit Abdul Samad and shows the Mushaf. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

What is kept on your device

  • Your settings (language, theme, colours, audio options).
  • Favourites, recently played recitations, where you stopped in each recitation, and Mushaf bookmarks.
  • A copy of the app itself, and Mushaf pages and translations you have opened, so they work offline.

This is stored only in your browser on your device (local storage and the browser's cache). It is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings or uninstalling the app.

Services the app connects to

To play audio and show pages, the app loads content from these services. They receive your device's IP address and standard browser information, as with any website you visit, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages (github.io): hosts the app.
  • Internet Archive (archive.org): live and radio recordings, and khatmas.
  • mp3quran.net: complete surah recordings.
  • verses.quran.com and everyayah.com: verse-by-verse audio in the Mushaf.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): Mushaf pages, Mushaf fonts and translations.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): the app's fonts.
  • YouTube: only when you open a khatma that plays from a YouTube playlist.

Sharing

When you share the app, a recitation or a verse, the app uses your phone's own share menu. A shared link only contains which recitation or verse it is and, if you choose, the time to start from.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For questions, use the project's page on GitHub: github.com/zainxali1390/tilawat-abdulbasit-adbusamad.
